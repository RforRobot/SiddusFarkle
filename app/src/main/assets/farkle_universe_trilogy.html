Official Scoring Rules & Chart

STRAIGHTS & SINGLES

  • Single 1: 100 points
  • Single 5: 50 points
  • 1–5 Straight (1-2-3-4-5): 500 points (leaves 1 die)
  • 2–6 Straight (2-3-4-5-6): 750 points (leaves 1 die)
  • 1–6 Straight (1-2-3-4-5-6): 1,500 points (triggers Hot Dice!)
  • No Three Pairs & No Two Triplets (strictly non-scoring).
  • Numbers 7 & 8: Pure dead weight; never score in straights or sets!

N-OF-A-KIND MULTIPLIERS

Base is 3-of-a-kind (1s = 1,000; 2s = 200; 3s = 300; 4s = 400; 5s = 500; 6s = 600).

  • 4-of-a-kind: 2× base value
  • 5-of-a-kind: 4× base value
  • 6-of-a-kind: 8× base value (Six 1s = 8,000 points!)
